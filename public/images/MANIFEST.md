## Notes / replacements made during curation

A number of initially shortlisted photos were downloaded, inspected pixel-by-pixel, and rejected in
favor of the picks above because they violated the brief's constraints:

- Rejected an Unsplash "bakery display case" shot for **bakery-main** — had extensive handwritten
  chalk-marker price/menu text on the glass.
- Rejected an "Only Cannoli" patisserie shot for **bakery-3** — visible shop brand name and price tags.
- Rejected a vintage bakery shot with a "La Ritz" branded jar sticker and price chalkboards.
- Rejected two candidates for **moment-afternoon** and one for **moment-midday** — both were shot
  inside a **Caffè Nero** location with the "CAFFÈ NERO" logo legible on the cup, saucer, and cast as a
  wall shadow.
- Rejected a wedding-reception cake shot (green tablecloth, ribbons) for **bakery-3** — didn't match
  the warm/dark boutique-café palette.
- Rejected a bright, white-background, Scandinavian-style bookshelf café for **cafe-experience** —
  too light/clinical and carried a visible wall decal ("Je veux, je peux.") plus a visible seated person.
- Rejected an original **cafe-experience** alternative with a wood bookshelf full of visible fashion
  magazine spines (Vogue, Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Bulgari, etc.) — competing luxury brand names.
- Swapped **signature-espresso** to a macro extraction shot after the first pick's background read as
  blown-out bright white rather than moody.

All final 18 selections were visually confirmed to have: no readable menu boards/price tags, no
competing brand logos, no faces as the main subject (hands/silhouettes only, and only in ritual-brew,
signature-slowbrew, and a small out-of-focus background figure in cafe-experience), and warm/dark
editorial lighting consistent with the "boutique European café" direction.
